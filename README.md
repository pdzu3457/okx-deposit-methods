## How to deposit USD with ACH

ACH is usually the most practical route for an eligible U.S. customer who wants to move money from a bank account without preparing a wire transfer manually.

OKX’s documented flow is:

1. Log in to your OKX account.
2. Open **Deposit** and select **Cash**.
3. Choose **USD** or **USDG**.
4. Select **Bank transfer (ACH)**.
5. Link a bank account through the available banking connection process, such as Plaid.
6. Enter the amount and open the preview.
7. Review the amount, fee, and receiving balance.
8. Submit the deposit.

OKX says eligible users can begin trading after the ACH deposit is completed. The money may still take additional time to leave the bank account, so a bank balance should be available after the transfer is initiated. The name on the OKX account should also match the name on the linked bank account.

ACH access is not universal across the United States. OKX states that USD deposits are limited to verified users who reside in eligible states. If the USD deposit option is missing, that does not necessarily mean the account is malfunctioning. It may simply mean the account, location, or verification profile does not qualify for that route.

### ACH is a good fit when

- You deposit from your own U.S. bank account.
- You want to fund an account for regular spot purchases.
- You do not need to send a large manual wire.
- You are comfortable linking a bank account and waiting for bank-side settlement.

Before confirming, check whether the deposit is going to your **USD balance** or **USDG balance**. These are separate selections in the documented OKX flow, so do not assume that choosing one automatically selects the other.

## How to deposit USD by domestic wire

A domestic wire is more suitable for a larger transfer or for users who prefer to enter the recipient and routing information directly through their bank.

To begin, open the OKX deposit page, select **US Dollar**, and choose **Domestic wire transfer**. OKX then provides the recipient name, address, routing number, account number, and other transfer instructions needed for your bank. You should copy those details directly from the deposit screen rather than relying on an old screenshot or saved banking template.

OKX states that it does not charge a fee for USD deposits through a U.S. domestic wire transfer, although the sending bank may apply its own charge. The documented processing estimate is generally **one to three business days**, depending on the bank. Transfers below the minimum or above the remaining maximum may be returned and can create additional bank charges.

The most common wire-transfer errors are simple:

- The bank account name does not match the verified OKX account name.
- A routing number or account number is copied incorrectly.
- The amount exceeds the remaining deposit limit.
- A transfer is sent using outdated instructions.
- The user forgets to check whether the deposit is available for their state or account.

For that reason, it is worth opening the deposit instructions immediately before sending the wire. Banking details can change, and the account-specific screen is the relevant source for the transaction.

OKX documents debit-card deposits for eligible U.S. users using a Visa or Mastercard issued by a U.S. bank. The card must be valid and may require verification before it can be used.

2. Choose **US Dollar**.
3. Select **Debit card**.
4. Add the card and enter the billing address.
5. Complete any card verification step.

7. Review the preview, including the fee and final credited amount.

A debit card can be convenient for a small or urgent deposit, but it is not automatically the cheapest route. The actual fee depends on the user’s location and method. The relevant figure is the amount shown in the preview immediately before submitting the transaction, not a generic fee displayed on a third-party comparison page.

## How to deposit crypto into OKX

Crypto deposits work differently from cash deposits. Instead of linking a bank account, you select an asset and network, copy an OKX deposit address, and send funds from another exchange or wallet.

The safe workflow is:

1. Open **Assets** and select **Deposit**.
2. Choose **Deposit crypto**.
3. Select the asset, such as BTC, ETH, or a supported stablecoin.
4. Select the correct network.
5. Copy the deposit address.
6. Copy the tag, memo, or comment if OKX displays one.
7. Open the withdrawal page on the sending exchange or wallet.
8. Select the same asset and the same network.
9. Paste the address and tag or memo.
10. Check the minimum deposit amount.
11. Send a small test transaction when the amount is significant or the route is unfamiliar.
12. Monitor the deposit status until the required blockchain confirmations are complete.

OKX specifically warns that the asset and network must match on both sides. If a token is sent through an unsupported network, the funds may not be credited and may be difficult or impossible to recover. The deposit page also displays the minimum amount and required confirmation information for the selected asset.

### Why the network choice matters

A token can exist on several networks. For example, the same stablecoin may be available through more than one blockchain, but the receiving address and sending network still need to be compatible.

Do not choose a network based only on the lower withdrawal fee shown by the sending platform. First confirm that OKX displays that same asset-network combination on its deposit page.

The cheapest-looking route can become the most expensive one if the funds are sent through an unsupported chain.

### When a tag or memo is required

Some assets use a shared deposit address and rely on a **tag**, **memo**, or **comment** to identify the recipient account. OKX explains that the tag is issued on its deposit page and must be entered together with the address when required. A missing or incorrect tag can prevent the deposit from being credited.

This is especially important when depositing assets whose exchange transfers commonly use an additional identifier. If the OKX deposit screen displays a tag or memo, copy it exactly. Do not enter random numbers, and do not assume that a tag from a previous transaction is still correct.

## Are OKX deposits free?

The answer depends on the deposit type.

### Crypto deposits

OKX says it does not charge a network fee when another party deposits crypto into your OKX account. The sending platform may still charge a withdrawal fee, and the blockchain transaction has to be processed by the network. In practice, the amount leaving the sending exchange can therefore be lower than the amount you intended to receive.

There is also a minimum deposit issue. If the amount sent is below the minimum shown on the OKX deposit page, the assets may not be credited automatically. Recovery may be available for some assets, but OKX says additional recovery fees can apply.

### Cash deposits

Cash deposit fees vary by method. OKX describes possible fee structures as:

- A flat fee.
- A percentage fee.
- A percentage plus a flat fee.
- No stated fee for a particular method.

The amount shown on the deposit preview is the number that matters. OKX explains that the fee may be deducted from the deposit amount itself. For example, if you submit a deposit of $100 and the method charges $2, the credited amount may be $98.

Do not confuse a deposit fee with a trading fee. Trading fees apply when you buy, sell, or otherwise execute a trade. Deposit fees, card processing charges, bank fees, and blockchain withdrawal fees are separate costs. OKX publishes separate fee information for trading tiers, while the deposit screen handles the method-specific cash charge.

## Deposit limits and processing times

OKX does not publish one universal minimum and maximum that applies to every user and every route. The limits can depend on:

- Deposit method.
- Account verification level.
- Region.
- Currency.
- Rolling daily or monthly limits.
- The number of deposits allowed within a defined period.

The cash deposit interface is designed to show the remaining limit for the selected method. OKX describes minimum transaction limits, daily limits, monthly limits, and maximum deposit counts as separate controls. Some limits reset on a rolling basis rather than at midnight.

Processing time also varies:

- A debit-card deposit can be available for trading after successful completion.
- An ACH deposit may be available for trading after completion while the bank-side debit takes longer.
- A domestic wire generally takes one to three business days.
- A crypto deposit waits for the required blockchain confirmations.
- Other bank or international routes depend on the sending and receiving institutions.

The practical rule is straightforward: read the displayed processing estimate and remaining limit after selecting the method. A general article cannot replace the account-specific information shown in the deposit preview.

## Why an OKX deposit may not appear

If you cannot find the method you expected, check these conditions:

1. **Identity verification is incomplete.** OKX requires verification for crypto deposits and limits cash methods according to local requirements.
2. **Your region is not eligible.** USD ACH, domestic wire, and debit-card deposits are limited to eligible U.S. users and states.
3. **The payment method is unsupported.** OKX only displays cash methods available for your region and account.
4. **The account is restricted or frozen.** A restricted account may have limited deposit functionality.
5. **The asset or network is unsupported.** If the token-network combination is missing from the crypto deposit page, do not send it.
6. **The amount exceeds the remaining limit.** The method may still be available, but the requested amount may not be accepted.

## A practical checklist before you confirm

Before submitting an OKX deposit, verify the following:

- The account has completed the required identity verification.
- The funding bank account or card is in your own name.
- The currency is correct.
- The deposit method is available for your location.
- The fee is acceptable.
- The final credited amount matches your expectation.

- For crypto, the asset and network match exactly.
- A tag, memo, or comment has been included when required.
- The sending platform shows the correct recipient address.
- You have saved the transaction ID or bank reference after submitting.

For larger crypto transfers, a small test transaction is usually worth the extra step. It costs a little time, but it can expose a network or memo error before the full balance is sent.
